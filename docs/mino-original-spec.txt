1. Main concept and purpose
MINO — Personal Life Journal and Productivity System
Project Overview
Build a beautiful, interactive, responsive personal journal website called Mino.

Mino is my personal life management system. Its purpose is to help me break up with doom scrolling, develop valuable skills, improve myself, manage my time, and appreciate life outside my phone.

I want to document my growth and make meaningful progress every day without sacrificing my personal happiness.

The website must balance three important aspects of my life:
1. Personal Development: Learning, skill-building, career preparation, finance, AI, and technology.
2. Physical and Personal Growth: Fitness, appearance, health habits, confidence, and self-care.
3. Living Life: Socializing, hobbies, exploring, reading, watching meaningful content, relaxing, and enjoying my time offline.
The website must not become another source of screen addiction. It should encourage me to spend less time on my phone and more time experiencing the real world.

The overall experience should feel like a cozy digital diary combined with a sophisticated productivity dashboard.

Mino's Personality and Instructions
Hello! I'm Mino!

I'm your daily buddy, and I'm here to make your days easier, more meaningful, and more productive.

I want to get to know you better, understand your interests, listen to your thoughts, and help you navigate your everyday life.

I know you're working on something important: breaking up with doom scrolling and discovering the world outside your phone.

I want to help you make the most of your time.

My responsibilities include:
- Greet you every day with a warm and engaging message.
- Ask how you're feeling and how your day is going.
- Help you organize your daily tasks.
- Remind you of your weekly and monthly goals.
- Encourage consistency without making you feel guilty.
- Celebrate even small accomplishments.
- Listen when you want to vent, reflect, or share random thoughts.
- Suggest meaningful activities when you're bored.
- Encourage you to socialize, explore, read, exercise, and spend time offline.
- Help you reflect on your screen time and digital habits.
- Recognize patterns in your productivity, mood, and routines.
I should never make you feel like your worth depends on productivity.

If you miss a task, don't shame me. Help me understand what happened and start again.

Sometimes I need motivation. Sometimes I need advice. Sometimes I just need someone to listen.

Remember: The objective isn't to make every minute productive. It's to make every day meaningful.

Let's make life happen outside the screen!

Mino should have the following features:

16. Technical and functional requirements
This section is especially important. I want an actual functioning website, not just a visual prototype.
Platform and accessibility
- Fully responsive on desktop, tablet, and mobile.
- Mobile-first interface.
- Installable Progressive Web App (PWA).
- Support Android and iOS browsers.
- Add-to-home-screen capability.
- Persistent user data.
- Fast loading and optimized assets.
- Accessible forms and navigation.
I want to access my journal through my phone wherever I am.
Data and backend
Implement:
- Secure authentication.
- Persistent database.
- User-specific data isolation.
- Automatic saving of journal entries.
- Image upload and storage.
- Daily, weekly, and monthly data aggregation.
- Backup and data export.
- Ability to edit and delete records.
- Timezone-aware date tracking using Asia/Manila.
Never store sensitive API keys in frontend code.
AI integration
Mino must use a real AI backend integration, not hardcoded responses.

Implement:
- Persistent conversation history.
- User-approved memory.
- Access to relevant goal and journal context.
- Personalized daily suggestions.
- Weekly and monthly summaries.
- Clear privacy controls.
- Ability to delete AI conversation history.
The AI should only access the information necessary for its task.
Suggested architecture
The coding agent may choose the best suitable technology stack, but I prefer:
- Frontend: React or Next.js.
- Language: TypeScript.
- Styling: Tailwind CSS.
- Components: shadcn/ui with customized styling.
- Backend/database: Supabase.
- Authentication: Supabase Auth.
- Charts: Recharts.
- AI: Secure server-side API integration with a suitable LLM provider.
- Hosting: Vercel or another suitable hosting provider.
Do not sacrifice functionality for design.
Important development requirements
1. Build actual working forms, buttons, checklists, and navigation.
2. Persist all important information across sessions and devices.
3. Ensure daily tasks reset correctly without erasing history.
4. Calculate weekly and monthly analytics from actual records.
5. Use real database relationships rather than temporary frontend arrays.
6. Add loading, empty, and error states.
7. Validate all user inputs.
8. Protect private journal entries and uploaded images.
9. Provide a backup and export mechanism.
10. Test the website on mobile and desktop.
Do not use fake progress data in the production version. Sample data may be used for initial previews, but it must be clearly identified as demo data.

Build the website in functional stages, and verify each feature before proceeding.

18. How I recommend building it
Don't ask the coding AI to build everything in one giant step. It may create a beautiful interface but leave important functions unfinished.

Use this implementation order:
1. Phase 1 — Foundation
   
   Build the database, authentication, responsive layout, navigation, design system, and dashboard.
2. Phase 2 — Daily Journal
   
   Implement daily checklist, mood tracker, journal, photo uploads, and calendar.
3. Phase 3 — Goal Management
   
   Implement all seven goals, milestones, weekly and monthly targets, and progress tracking.
4. Phase 4 — Analytics
   
   Build the GitHub-style activity calendar, screen time tracker, savings tracker, and productivity charts.
5. Phase 5 — AI Buddy
   
   Integrate Mino, conversational memory, personalized recommendations, and weekly reflections.
6. Phase 6 — Advanced Features
   
   Build Tech Lab, content library, Me Time, vision board, achievements, and yearly memories.
7. Phase 7 — Deployment
   
   Test everything, optimize mobile responsiveness, enable PWA installation, configure secure storage, and deploy the actual website.

My final recommendations
There are three decisions I'd strongly recommend making before the coding AI starts.

1. Make it a PWA. This means you can install Mino on your phone's home screen and use it almost like a native application without needing to build separate Android and iOS apps.

2. Make your data persistent from day one. Your journal, pictures, mood history, and progress should not disappear when you change devices or refresh the website.

3. Keep Mino and Tech Buddy separate. Mino should handle your personal life, routines, and goals. Tech Buddy should focus on technical exploration, coding, AI, Linux, and reverse engineering. They can share relevant project progress, but their personalities and responsibilities should remain distinct.

One more thing: I would make your Me Time and offline experiences just as visible as your AI and trading goals. Your goal isn't simply to become more productive. It's to have a richer life.

A little progress every day, but never at the expense of living.
